Quick Tips

Bite-sized advice you can apply today.

Hook Formula for Fitness Reels

Start with a bold claim or question in the first 0.5 seconds. Example: "This 3-minute routine burns more than 30 minutes on the treadmill." Pattern-interrupt hooks outperform generic intros by 3x.

Best Times to Post Fitness Content

Based on aggregate data: weekdays at 6–7 AM and 5–7 PM local time perform best. Weekends peak at 8–10 AM. Always test with your own audience and adjust.

The 80/20 Rule for Fitness Creators

80% value content (tips, demos, education). 20% promotional content (product links, coaching offers, challenge signups). This ratio builds trust without feeling salesy.
